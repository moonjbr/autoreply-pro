FAQ

Common questions

Do I need technical skills to set this up?
No. Setup takes about 3 minutes. You just enter your business name, add some FAQs, and you're live. No coding required.
Which channels does AutoReply Pro support?
Currently we support Instagram DMs, WhatsApp Business, Email (Gmail), and Google Business. More channels are being added regularly.
Can I see what the AI replies before it sends?
Yes. You can enable "Draft mode" in Settings — the AI writes a reply for you to review and approve before it's sent.
What happens if the AI doesn't know the answer?
You can set it to escalate to you after a certain number of turns, or it will politely let the customer know you'll follow up personally.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel anytime from your billing page with one click.
Is my customer data safe?
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models.
