FAQ

Common questions

Do I need technical skills to set this up?
No. Setup takes about 3 minutes. You enter your business info, add some FAQs, and you're live. No coding required whatsoever.
Which channels are supported?
Instagram DMs, WhatsApp Business, Email (Gmail), and Google Business. More channels are being added as we grow.
Can I review AI replies before they're sent?
Yes. Enable "Draft mode" in Settings and the AI will write a suggested reply for you to approve before anything is sent to the customer.
What if the AI doesn't know the answer?
You can set it to escalate to you after a set number of turns, or it will politely let the customer know you will follow up personally.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel anytime from your billing page with one click. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
Is my customer data safe?
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell your data or use customer conversations to train AI models.
